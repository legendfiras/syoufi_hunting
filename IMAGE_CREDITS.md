# Images

The circular logo and outdoor hero are the supplied Syoufi Hunting artwork:

- `public/images/syoufi/logo.png` — circular badge extracted from the profile screenshot, with transparency outside the circle
- `public/images/syoufi/hero-banner.jpg` — outdoor banner, original JPEG bytes
- `public/images/syoufi/favicon-16.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-48.png`, `favicon-192.png`, `favicon-512.png`
- `public/images/syoufi/apple-touch-icon.png`

Product photographs in `public/images/products/` were downloaded from the manufacturer pages listed in `src/content/product-image-manifest.ts`. They are for this local demo. Reuse permission has not been verified, and the files are not cleared for publication. Products that never received a photo were removed from the catalog. Brand artwork is not used as a product image.

- Hunting clothing uses the Sea to Summit poncho photograph.
